Offclue support

Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Developer: Aleksandr Paradeev

On-device play

Offclue works without an account or internet connection. The app does not send player names, roles, words, or voting history to the developer. It contains no ads, analytics, in-app purchases, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Settings and the current game, including player names, characters, pattern keys, roles, words, votes, and scores, are stored locally so that play can resume. Names of players who completed rounds can also be saved for a later game, without individual statistics. Players may use fictional names.

Deleting data

End the game to remove its private game data. You can remove individual saved names or clear the name list in settings. Deleting the app removes its local data. If iOS backup is enabled, backups are managed separately through Apple and can be removed in device settings.

Motion and feedback

Device tilt is used only for the optional parallax effect. A system timer helps coordinate interface feedback. These measurements are not stored or sent to the developer. Parallax, vibration, and sounds can be disabled in settings. The app does not record speech, photos, or video.

Contact

If you choose to email support@paradeyev.com, your email address and message are used to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords or other players’ real names.
