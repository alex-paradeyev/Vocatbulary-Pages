Need help with Offclue? Contact the developer at support@paradeyev.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Avoid sending passwords or other players’ real names.

Quick help

Offclue is played together on one device. Choose players and categories, pass the device privately to reveal roles, give clues, and vote. Interface language and word language can be selected independently in settings.

Saved player names can be removed individually or cleared in settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; iOS backups are managed separately in your device settings.
